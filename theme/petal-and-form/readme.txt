A soft, editorial block theme for independent makers and boutique shops.

== Description ==

Petal & Form is a pastel, editorial block theme for small creative businesses — ceramics, candles,
stationery, jewelry, gifts and home goods. It includes three curated style variations
(Blush Atelier, Sage & Oat, Lavender Studio), storytelling patterns for makers, a Pinterest-inspired
inspiration board, a gift-finder landing page and styled WooCommerce templates.

Everything is built from core and WooCommerce blocks and is editable in the Site Editor.
The theme ships no JavaScript of its own and loads no external resources.

WooCommerce is optional. Without it, product sections are simply omitted.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme.
2. Upload petal-and-form.zip and click Install Now, then Activate.
3. Optional: install and activate WooCommerce for the store templates.
4. Go to Appearance > Editor > Styles to choose a style variation.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does the theme include a newsletter or contact form? =

No. The theme does not collect personal data. The newsletter and contact patterns are designed
to hold the block of a form or newsletter plugin of your choice.

= Are the shipping, returns and legal patterns legal advice? =

No. They are layouts with placeholder guidance. Requirements differ between countries.
